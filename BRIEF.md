## The problem
- Many People struggle with not knowing what to wear for the day because there are lots of cloths in your wardrobe and some might get ignored or you don't know how to pair your cloths.
- Impact area: Something else
- Why it matters (impact): We can make sure people don't have a dilemma over what to where and have clothes just sitting there without being worn.
- Evidence it’s real: University of Minnesota: Having lots of clothes doesn’t always make choosing outfits easy.

The university’s Smart Wardrobe research page explains that people can struggle to put clothes together even when their wardrobe contains many possible combinations. Researchers are exploring technology that suggests outfits using clothes people already own.
- What people told me: Person A : "Some of items in my closet, I can't see, so I forget about it."
Person B : "Sometimes it takes too much energy in the morning to match and find cloths to wear."
## My solution
- My AI’s job: My AI helps by telling humans what cloths to wear for today, which works better than using your brainpower in the morning and not knowing what to wear because it can pair cloths that you should wear for the day every fast.
- Tools that already exist, and how mine is different: Clothes Matchers already exist I'll make one that also reminds people where their cloths are and have them take a full body picture of themselves so they don't have to try clothes on and offf to see how it looks
- Safety plan: Risk: there is personal info/photos
Plan: have a maniquin if they don't want to use their photo, and ensure photos dont get shared
## My MVP features (build these first, in this order)
1. The user can ask the AI for today's outfit from their saved clothes.
2. The user can photograph each piece of clothing to add it to their wardrobe.
3. The user can see the suggested outfit shown on their photo or a mannequin.
4. The AI gives correct, safe answers to real questions.
## Maybe later (not yet)
- The user can choose a mannequin instead of using their own photo.
- The user can note where each piece of clothing is stored.
- The user can tell the AI the weather or event to match the outfit.
- The user can mark an outfit as worn so the AI suggests forgotten clothes.
- The user can save outfits they like to a favorites list.
- The user can reject an outfit and get a new AI pairing.
- The user can delete their photos from the app at any time.
- The user can see which clothes they have not worn in a month.
## My AI’s instructions
- When it answers: The user presses a button that says make an outfit for me. The ai makes an outfit
- Its job: To match clothes for the occasion
- Who it talks to and how to sound: THey are talking to the user and should sound friendly and give helpful suggestions
- Facts it must use: The clothes the user has saved, including each item’s name, type, and main color.
The user’s chosen style preferences.
Basic outfit-matching principles, such as coordinating colors and combining clothing styles.
Only clothes the user owns—it must not invent items.
- Rules it must follow: It must never insult someone or take someones privacy
- How to answer: Show the suggested outfit first, using pictures of the user’s saved clothes. Keep words to a minimum—only clothing names. If something goes wrong, show one short, friendly sentence explaining what to do next.
